Contact form

If you send a message, I store your name, email address, optional subject and message in a Firebase database (a Google service). I use them only to reply to you. They stay there until I delete them.

Analytics

Analytics cookies are off until you accept them in the cookie banner. If you accept, Google Analytics (through Firebase) counts visits and clicks on links and buttons. You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" in the footer of the home page.

Stored in your browser

Your theme choice (dark or light) and your cookie choice are saved in your browser so the site remembers them. They are not used to track you.

What I do not do

I do not sell your data, show ads, or share your messages with anyone.

Your choices

Email me to see or delete a message you sent, and I will remove it.
